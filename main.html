About Luméa Beauty Studios

Luméa Beauty Studios is a modern beauty studio dedicated to creating refined, personalized beauty experiences in a warm and elegant setting.

We focus on enhancing each client’s natural beauty through thoughtful services, attention to detail, and professional care.

From the moment you arrive, our goal is to make you feel comfortable, confident, and beautifully cared for.

What sets Luméa apart is our balance of luxury and personal connection, creating an experience that feels both sophisticated and welcoming.

Our Services

Hair Styling

Express your style with beautifully crafted braids created with attention to detail, comfort, and a lasting, polished finish.

Manicure & Pedicure

We provide professional manicure and pedicure services that keep your hands and feet beautifully groomed, polished, and refreshed.

Lash Installation

We create customized lash looks at Luméa, carefully selecting and installing lashes to enhance your eyes and complement your overall style.

Wig Installation

We provide professional wig installation for a secure, comfortable, and polished finish that complements your look and personal style.

Massages

Luméa offers relaxing massage experiences designed to help you unwind, release everyday tension, and enjoy a moment of calm and comfort.

Facial treatments

Our facial treatments are designed to care for your skin while leaving you feeling refreshed, radiant, and confidently ready to glow.
